The notch has three sizes. Closed, it blends into the camera housing and shows [live activities](/live-activities/) beside it. A [glance](/glance/) shows what's live, and the open notch has everything.

<Shot name="10-open-nook" eager alt="The open notch on the Nook tab, showing the music player beside the next calendar event." />

## Opening it

| Do this | And |
|---|---|
| Rest the pointer on the notch | A glance grows out of it |
| Click the notch, or the glance | The whole notch opens |
| Swipe down on the trackpad over the notch | The whole notch opens |
| Press <Keys k="⌃⌥N" /> | The notch opens, or closes if it's open |
| Press <Keys k="⌃⌥K" /> | The notch opens on the [command field](/command-field/) |
| Drag files toward the notch | It opens on the [Tray's drop zones](/tray/) |
| Choose **Open Notch** or **Open Tray** in the menu bar menu | The notch opens |
| Run `dynotch open`, or open a [`dynotch://` link](/links/) | The notch opens on the tab you name |

## Closing it

- **Move the pointer away.** If you opened it without the pointer, with a shortcut, the menu bar or a link, it waits until the pointer has been over it once.
- **Click anywhere outside it.**
- **Swipe up.** Over a list with more than it shows, the swipe scrolls the list, so on the Focus tab only the header closes it. Over the Nook's stack, the swipe turns the stack.
- **Press <Keys k="⌃⌥N" /> again.**
- **Press <Keys k="Esc" />,** whichever app you're in. While the notch is open the key is the notch's, so the app doesn't get it. A menu, a drag or AirPlay's device list still gets it first.

## Moving between tabs

The open notch has four tabs: **Nook**, **Tray**, **Focus** and **Control**. Click one, or swipe two fingers sideways over the open notch. Over the Tray's strip of files, once it runs over, the swipe scrolls the strip instead. The Nook is always there, and you can hide the others in <Path to="Settings → Nook & Widgets → Tabs" />.

Over the header, the same sideways swipe skips tracks instead, as it does over the closed notch. See [Audio and keys](/audio-keys/).

## Changing how it opens

All of this is in <Path to="Settings → General → Opening the notch" />.

| Setting | What it does | Starts as |
|---|---|---|
| Hover | A glance, or the whole notch, when the pointer rests on it | On |
| Swipe | Swipe down to open, up to close, sideways to skip a track or change tabs | On |
| Keyboard shortcut | Toggles the notch with <Keys k="⌃⌥N" /> | On |
| Haptic feedback | A tap from the trackpad when the notch responds | On |
| Hover shows | **A glance**, or **The full notch** as Dynotch did before it had glances | A glance |
| Hover delay | How long the pointer rests before anything happens, from 0 to 1 second | 0.15 s |
