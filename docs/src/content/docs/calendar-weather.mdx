## Calendar

The Calendar widget, in the [Nook](/dynotch/nook/), shows what's up next: the next event in full, with "in 12m" or "until 11:00", its times and call service, and **Open in Calendar**. The rest of the day runs underneath. After the day's last event, it shows tomorrow's first.

<Shot name="98-open-nook-shortcuts-calendar" eager alt="The Calendar widget beside the player: Design review in 12 minutes, with Join and Open in Calendar buttons, and two more events today." />

**Join** appears for Zoom, Meet, Teams, Webex, FaceTime, Whereby, GoToMeeting and Amazon Chime links.

Five minutes before an event, a notice shows in the closed notch. Within 15 minutes of one, the stack in the Nook turns to the calendar by itself, if the calendar is one of your two widgets.

<Shot name="08-closed-event" alt="The closed notch saying Design review is in five minutes." />

Turn off **Show all-day events** in <Path to="Settings → Nook & Widgets → Calendar" /> to leave them out. The widget needs the Calendar permission.

## Weather

The Weather widget shows current conditions and today's high and low, from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), which needs no account or key.

<Shot name="93-open-nook-weather-notes" alt="The Weather widget for Lisbon: 65 degrees, partly cloudy, high 70 and low 55, with rain in 20 minutes." />

Set it up in <Path to="Settings → Nook & Widgets → Weather" />:

| Setting | What it does | Starts as |
|---|---|---|
| City | Search for a city | None |
| Use my location | Uses where your Mac is instead. It needs the Location permission | Off |
| Temperature | **Like the system**, **Celsius** or **Fahrenheit** | Like the system |
| Rain alerts | A heads-up in the closed notch when rain is about to start | On |

### Rain alerts

When rain is about to start, a heads-up appears in the closed notch, and the stack turns to the weather if that's one of your two widgets. The alert comes from Open-Meteo's 15-minute forecast where there is one, and the hourly forecast elsewhere.
