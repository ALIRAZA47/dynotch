## Gestures

| Gesture | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Swipe two fingers left | The closed notch, or the open notch's header | Next track |
| Swipe two fingers right | The closed notch, or the open notch's header | Previous track |
| Scroll | The artwork in the Nook | Changes the volume |

Over the rest of the open notch, the same sideways swipe moves between tabs, unless what's under the pointer scrolls sideways itself, like the Tray's strip of files once it runs over.

## Outputs

The output button beside the player lists the Mac's outputs (speakers, headphones, displays, Bluetooth) and switches the default output. AirPlay speakers and TVs come from AVKit's AirPlay picker in the same list.

<Shot name="81-open-nook-outputs" eager alt="The Output list over the player, showing MacBook Pro Speakers, AirPods Pro with a check mark and Studio Display Speakers, with an AirPlay button." />

The Control tab has a menu of the same outputs under its sound slider, without AirPlay.

## Live equalizer

The equalizer bars are decorative until you turn on **Live equalizer** in <Path to="Settings → Live Activities → Equalizer and keys" />. Then a Core Audio process tap listens to Music or Spotify, only while it plays and some bars are on screen, and splits the sound into four bands.

It needs macOS 14.2 or later and the System Audio Recording permission. Nothing is recorded.

<Shot name="84-open-nook-live-levels" alt="The Nook with the equalizer bars following the music." />

## Replace system HUD

With **Replace system HUD** on, in the same section, Dynotch takes over the volume, mute and brightness keys, so only its meter shows, even at either end of the range.

<Shot name="06-closed-volume" alt="The closed notch as a volume meter." />

- Each press moves a sixteenth of the range, and a sixty-fourth with <Keys k="⇧⌥" /> held.
- Keys Dynotch can't handle, like an output without a volume control or an external display, go to macOS as usual.
- It needs Accessibility, which it asks for when you turn it on.
- It needs **Volume & brightness** on as well.

Without it, the volume and brightness HUD appears alongside the system one.
