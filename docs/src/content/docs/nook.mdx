The Nook is the first tab of the open notch, and the one that's always there.

## The player

The player follows Apple Music and Spotify. It shows the artwork, the track and where it's playing, a scrubber, a big play button, next and previous, shuffle and repeat, on a glow in the album art's color.

- **Favorite** is the star, for Apple Music. Spotify doesn't let other apps like songs.
- **The output button** lists the Mac's outputs and switches between them. See [Audio and keys](/audio-keys/#outputs).
- **Scrolling over the artwork** changes the volume.

With nothing playing, the player offers a button for each of the two that's installed.

<Shot name="11-open-nook-empty" alt="The Nook with nothing playing, offering buttons to start Music or Spotify." />

Turn off **Artwork tint** in <Path to="Settings → Appearance → Style" /> to color the player with your accent instead of the album art.

## The stack

Beside the player sits a stack of up to two widgets, one showing at a time. Choose them in <Path to="Settings → Nook & Widgets → Stacked beside the player" />.

| Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Calendar | Up next, and the rest of the day. See [Calendar and weather](/calendar-weather/) |
| Mirror | Your camera, to check yourself before a call. Click the card to turn it on and again to turn it off, and it stops by itself when the card leaves the screen |
| Shortcuts | Up to four pinned Shortcuts, a click away |
| Focus timer | The Pomodoro, with its controls |
| Batteries | Rings for the Mac, AirPods, keyboards, mice and more |
| Quick toggles | The Control tab's toggles |
| Notes | Your [notes](/focus/#notes), with their checklists |
| Weather | Current conditions, and today's high and low |

The first widget starts as Calendar and the second as Mirror. Either can be **Nothing**.

### Turning the stack

The stack turns by itself to whatever matters now, as long as that widget is one of your two:

- to the calendar during an event, or within 15 minutes of one
- to the weather when rain is on its way
- to the batteries when one runs low

To turn it by hand, swipe up or down over it, or use the dots on its edge. A card with more than it shows, like a long note or the batteries, scrolls first.

## Live equalizer

The bars beside the track are decorative until you turn on **Live equalizer** in <Path to="Settings → Live Activities" />. Then they follow the music. See [Audio and keys](/audio-keys/#live-equalizer).
