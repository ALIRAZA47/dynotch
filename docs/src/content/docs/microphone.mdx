## Muting the microphone

Press <Keys k="⌃⌥M" />, choose **Mute Microphone** in the menu bar menu, click the Control tab's **Mute mic** tile, or type `mute` in the command field. The mic mutes in every app, by its mute control, or by turning its input level down when it has none.

A red badge stays in the closed notch while it's muted, beside whatever else is showing. With **Two at once** off, it shows only when nothing else does.

The switches are in <Path to="Settings → Live Activities" />: **Muted microphone** for the badge, and **Mute shortcut** under **Options** for the shortcut.

## The on-air ring

A thin ring outlines the closed notch while a camera or the mic is in use: green for a camera, orange for the mic.

<Shots min={320}>
  <Shot name="33-closed-on-air-camera" alt="The closed notch outlined in green." caption="A camera is on" />
  <Shot name="34-closed-on-air-mic-muted" alt="The closed notch outlined in orange, with music and the mute badge." caption="The mic is in use" />
</Shots>

Like macOS's own indicator, it leaves out Siri listening for its name.

Turn it off with **On-air ring** in <Path to="Settings → Live Activities → Options" />.
