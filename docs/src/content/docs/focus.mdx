The Focus tab has two cards. The first holds the Pomodoro timer and the named timers. The second switches between Reminders and Notes.

<Shot name="14-open-focus" eager alt="The Focus tab: a Pomodoro dial at 18:24, three timers and a stopwatch, and a list of reminders." />

## The Pomodoro timer

Focus for a while, take a short break, and after a few sessions take a long one. The dial shows where you are, with the session's dots under the time. The buttons under it reset, start or pause, and skip to the next phase.

The rhythm is yours to change in <Path to="Settings → Focus Timer" />:

| Setting | Range | Starts as |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | 5 to 120 minutes | 25 min |
| Short break | 1 to 30 minutes | 5 min |
| Long break | 5 to 60 minutes | 15 min |
| Long break after | 2 to 8 sessions | 4 sessions |
| Auto-start | Starts the next phase by itself | Off |
| Sound | Plays a sound when a phase or a named timer ends | On |

<Shot name="settings-6-focus" themed alt="The Focus Timer page of Dynotch's Settings, with dials for each duration and switches for Auto-start and Sound." />

## Named timers

Beside the Pomodoro, type a name and a time to start a timer:

| Type | For |
|---|---|
| `tea 4m` | 4 minutes |
| `eggs 90s` | 90 seconds |
| `nap 1h30` | An hour and a half |
| `laundry 45` | 45 minutes: a number on its own is minutes |

Pause, resume or cancel each one. The closed notch shows the timer that ends soonest, the Pomodoro included, with the next one as a glyph beside it. At zero there's a notice and a sound.

<Shot name="40-closed-countdown" alt="The closed notch counting down Tea at 3:41." />

## The stopwatch

The stopwatch sits under the timers, and shows in the closed notch while it runs.

## Reminders

The second card lists your Reminders. Check them off, or add a new one at the bottom. It needs the Reminders permission.

<Shot name="97-open-focus-busy" alt="The Focus tab with four timers, and reminders including one overdue and one with a long title." />

## Notes

Switch the second card to **Notes** for several notes with Markdown checklists. Click a box to tick it, and Return continues the list.

<Shot name="14-open-focus-notes" alt="The Focus tab with a note called Weekend, a checklist with one item ticked off." />

Each note is saved as a `.md` file named after its first line. Choose where they live in <Path to="Settings → Nook & Widgets → Notes" />:

- **Use iCloud Drive** keeps them in a **Dynotch Notes** folder, so they sync to your other Macs.
- **Choose Folder…** puts them anywhere you like.
- **Use Dynotch's Folder** brings them back to `~/Library/Application Support/Dynotch/Notes`.

Changes made on disk show up right away.

<Note warn>Notes only move when you leave Dynotch's own folder. A folder you picked is left as it is, so switching away from it leaves your notes there, and the next folder starts without them. To bring them back, move the files yourself.</Note>

## From elsewhere

- `dynotch timer start` runs the Pomodoro from a script. See [The dynotch command](/dynotch/dynotch-command/#timer).
- The [command field](/dynotch/command-field/) starts timers and adds notes and reminders from anywhere.
