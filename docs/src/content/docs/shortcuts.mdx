## Anywhere

| Keys | What they do | Where to turn them off |
|---|---|---|
| <Keys k="⌃⌥N" /> | Opens or closes the notch | <Path to="Settings → General" /> |
| <Keys k="⌃⌥K" /> | Opens the command field | <Path to="Settings → General" /> |
| <Keys k="⌃⌥M" /> | Mutes or unmutes the microphone | <Path to="Settings → Live Activities" /> |
| <Keys k="⌃⌥S" /> | Captures part of the screen | <Path to="Settings → Tray" /> |

## Trackpad

Two fingers, with the pointer over the notch.

| Gesture | Over | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Swipe down | The notch or glance | Opens the notch |
| Swipe up | The open notch's header, anywhere without a scrolling list, or a glance | Closes it |
| Swipe left | The closed notch, or the open notch's header | Next track |
| Swipe right | The closed notch, or the open notch's header | Previous track |
| Swipe sideways | The rest of the open notch | Moves between tabs |
| Swipe up or down | The Nook's stack | Turns the stack |
| Scroll | The artwork in the Nook | Changes the volume |

**Swipe** in <Path to="Settings → General" /> turns off the swipes, except for turning the stack. Scrolling over the artwork stays too.

## The open notch

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| <Keys k="Esc" /> | Closes it or a glance, whichever app you're in. A menu, a drag or AirPlay's device list gets the key first |

## The command field

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| <Keys k="↑ ↓" /> | Picks a row |
| <Keys k="Return" /> | Runs it |
| <Keys k="Esc" /> | Clears what's typed, and then leaves |

## The Tray

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| <Keys k="Space" /> | Previews the selected file in Quick Look |
| Arrow keys | Move between files |

## Capture

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| <Keys k="P" /> <Keys k="L" /> <Keys k="A" /> <Keys k="R" /> <Keys k="C" /> | Pencil, Line, Arrow, Rectangle, Ellipse |
| <Keys k="M" /> <Keys k="T" /> <Keys k="B" /> <Keys k="N" /> | Marker, Text, Blur, Counter |
| <Keys k="S" /> | Back to moving the selection |
| <Keys k="⌘Z" /> | Undoes |
| <Keys k="⇧⌘Z" /> | Redoes |
| <Keys k="⌘C" /> | Copies |
| <Keys k="⌘S" /> | Saves |
| <Keys k="Return" /> | Does what **After capture** says |
| <Keys k="Esc" /> | Cancels |

On a pinned capture, <Keys k="Esc" /> or <Keys k="⌘W" /> closes it.

## Settings

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| <Keys k="⌘1" /> to <Keys k="⌘8" /> | Jump to a page |
| <Keys k="↑ ↓" /> | Move through the pages, once the sidebar is selected |

<Path to="Settings → About → Keyboard" /> lists the main ones, in case you forget.
