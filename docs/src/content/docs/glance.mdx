title: Glance
description: Rest the pointer on the notch for what's live, with a control or two.
---

Resting the pointer on the closed notch grows it into a glance. It's enough to pause the music or check a timer without opening anything, and it goes away when you move off it.

<Shot name="glance-1-music-crop" eager alt="The glance on a track, with the artwork, the title, a scrubber, and previous, pause and next buttons." />

## What it shows

It follows the closed notch, with the controls that go with whatever's there.

| The closed notch shows | The glance shows |
|---|---|
| Music, or a song's title | The track, with play, pause and skip |
| The Pomodoro | The time left, with skip and pause |
| A named timer | The time left, with +1 min and pause |
| The stopwatch | The time so far, with reset and pause |
| A download | Its name and progress |
| A notice | The notice |

## With the notch empty

With nothing in the closed notch but perhaps a volume or brightness meter, the glance takes the first of these:

1. A timed event that's on now or starts within 15 minutes, with **Join** if it has a call link.
2. A track that's loaded, even a paused one.
3. The day: the date, the battery, the weather, the next event and the Tray, each a click from its tab.

<Shot name="glance-8-today" alt="The glance showing Friday 30, the battery, the next event and five files in the Tray." />

## Getting out of it

A click anywhere else on the glance, or a swipe down, opens the whole notch. A swipe up or moving away closes it.

## Turning it off

<Path to="Settings → General → Hover shows" /> brings back the old behavior: **The full notch** opens the whole thing on hover, with no glance in between.
